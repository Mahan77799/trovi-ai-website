How we think

Industry first. Technology second.

A restaurant reservation call is not a dental patient call. A salon reschedule is not a tax consultation. The product should reflect the business instead of forcing the business into one generic script.

Practical automation

Automate the repetitive parts that create real operational drag.

Specific workflows

Build around the rules, services and handoffs of the industry.

Transparent promises

Verify integrations and avoid claims we cannot prove.

Human when needed

Escalation is a feature, not a failure.

Why Trovi exists

The customer should not lose because the team is busy.

Customers call during rushes, after hours and while staff are already helping someone else. Trovi is designed to cover that gap without turning the business into a software project.
