What we verify

The integration has to complete a real job.

A logo on a website means nothing if it cannot perform the exact action your call flow needs. We map the action, the source of truth and the fallback first.

01

What is the action?

Book, reschedule, cancel, look up availability, create a lead, send a message or notify a person.

02

Where is the source of truth?

Your booking platform, calendar, CRM, inbox or internal process determines what can safely be automated.

03

What happens if it fails?

A useful system needs a fallback: capture the request, notify the team or transfer instead of dropping the call.

Our rule

We do not promise an integration until we know it exists.

Some platforms expose rich APIs. Others expose only limited actions, and some expose no suitable connection for the workflow you need. Trovi is designed around that reality.

Why this matters: forcing a business to replace a working system just to use phone automation defeats the point. The implementation should reduce operational friction, not create a second system to maintain.
