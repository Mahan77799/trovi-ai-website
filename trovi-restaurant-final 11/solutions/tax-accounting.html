Straight answers

What accounting-firm owners ask first.

We configure the call flow around the firm's service model and client boundaries.

Can Trovi answer tax questions?

Only administrative or general process information that your firm has explicitly approved. Questions requiring judgement, interpretation or advice are routed to a professional.

Can it qualify new clients?

Yes. You choose the intake questions and qualification rules, such as service type, entity type, deadline, location or minimum engagement criteria. Trovi captures the answers before the consult or handoff.

Can it tell clients what documents are needed?

It can provide a firm-approved checklist or process instructions. It should not decide which documents are legally or technically sufficient for a specific tax position unless that logic is explicitly supplied by your firm.

Can it work outside tax season?

Yes. The same workflow can support bookkeeping leads, advisory consultations, year-round client questions, appointment scheduling and administrative call routing.
