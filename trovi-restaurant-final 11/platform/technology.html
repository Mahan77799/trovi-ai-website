Your phone stops being a queue. It becomes a workflow.

From conversation to action

The value is what happens after “hello.”

A useful call workflow has four parts: understand the request, check the rules, act where the system allows it, and preserve context when a person takes over.

1
Understand the reason for the callThe conversation determines what the caller is trying to accomplish.
2
Use business-approved informationHours, services, policies, pricing guidance and process information come from the business.
3
Take an approved actionWhere supported and configured, workflows can book, message, text, capture information or call connected tools and APIs.
4
Escalate with contextWhen the call needs a person, the goal is a useful handoff, not making the customer restart from zero.
