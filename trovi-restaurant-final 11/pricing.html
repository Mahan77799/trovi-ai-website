Pricing

Pricing should match the phone traffic. Not punish the small shop.

Trovi pricing is based on expected call volume and the service level the business needs. A business getting ten calls a week should not be priced like one handling hundreds.

Why we price this way

One flat price for every business is easy to explain and usually unfair.

Phone demand varies dramatically by business type, location, season and operating model. The quote should reflect the load and the workflow you are actually asking Trovi to handle.
