The operating model

Rules before improvisation.

Trovi should know what it is allowed to answer, what it is allowed to do and when a human needs to take over. That boundary is part of setup.

01

Approved knowledge

Hours, services, policies, pricing guidance and FAQs come from information the business provides and approves.

02

Approved actions

Booking, rescheduling, messaging, texting and transfers are configured around the workflow you actually want.

03

Escalation rules

When a call needs judgement, urgency or a person, the workflow can route or capture it instead of pretending.

04

Test before launch

We run the scenarios that matter to your business before the line becomes customer-facing.
