The real salon problem

The phone rings when your team is least able to stop.

Most salon demand is time-sensitive. A caller wants to know whether you do a service, what it costs, who is available, or whether they can move an appointment. If the answer takes too long, the booking can disappear before it ever reaches the book.

01 · HANDS BUSY

The stylist cannot pause mid-service.

Calls arrive while someone is cutting, colouring, shampooing or checking out another client. Trovi answers without turning the client in the chair into second priority.

02 · EMPTY SLOT

A cancellation creates a hole fast.

When a client cancels, the useful window to reschedule or refill that time is short. Trovi can handle the cancellation, offer approved alternatives and trigger the follow-up your workflow supports.

03 · AFTER HOURS

Booking intent does not stop at closing.

People search salons after work, on weekends and whenever they finally have time. Trovi can answer common questions and capture or book the request instead of sending it to voicemail.

Straight answers

What salon owners usually want to know.

The workflow is configured around your service menu and booking rules. We do not pretend every salon runs the same way.

Can Trovi book directly into my salon software?

Where your platform supports a suitable integration, we can connect the workflow. If it does not, we build a compatible booking or handoff process around the tools you already use instead of promising an integration that does not exist.

Can it answer detailed service questions?

Yes, when the answer is based on information your salon provides and approves: services, starting prices, durations, preparation, policies and availability rules. Anything that requires a stylist's judgement can be routed to your team.

Can it handle cancellations and reschedules?

Yes. Trovi can process those requests according to your rules and the capabilities of your booking workflow, then send the appropriate confirmation or team notification.

Will it replace my receptionist?

It does not have to. Many salons need help most when everyone is already serving a client, during overflow or after hours. Trovi can be configured around those gaps rather than replacing the people who already know your clients.
